Research

Research

I work on AI for engineering design whose output an engineer can open, check and change. Two papers are published, one is in preparation, and three systems have been built with industry.

1Direction

My research question is how to give design software an explicit, verifiable representation of a product's engineering state: its variables, the dependencies between them and the limits within which the design stays valid. Engineers and AI agents can share such a representation, inspect it, and check it against geometry, physics and standards.

A CAD file records geometry and, at best, the feature history that produced it. The reasons behind a dimension, such as the clearance a standard requires, usually live outside the file. An edit made by a language model then has nothing to be checked against. I call the missing layer a verifiable intermediate representation, and each project below works on one part of it.

  • Recovering it from existing parts. Abra-CAD rebuilds an editable CAD program from a mesh, point cloud, B-rep, drawing or render.
  • Editing it safely. In the TE busbar system, engineers and an LLM edit the same design state, and a geometry solver checks every change before CAD regenerates.
  • Checking a made part against it. ABI inspection places detections on the part's CAD model, and SynthDefect-CAD compiles public standards into rules over CAD regions.
  • The data underneath. MM-CAD aligns five descriptions of each of 226K CAD models.

4In preparation

  • Abra-CAD: Fit What Is Observed, Learn What Is Missing, Search What Remains

    A. Bharathi, R. Muthuganapathy

    In preparation for CVPR 2027

    Editable CAD programs from meshes, point clouds, B-reps, drawings and renders. On about 5,000 parts with known construction programs, structure recovery returns 92.5% of pattern and instance counts, 97.5% of hole diameters and 95.0% of fillet radii.

    Project page on this site

  • SynthDefect-CAD

    Research in progress, ADOPT Lab, IIT Madras

    Inspection as acceptance against a named standard and quality level, with rules compiled over regions of the part's CAD model. The benchmark covers eight part families that are cast, forged or machined, and is under construction.

    Project page on this site
